## Overview

_Placeholder text drafted from the resume._ As responsible engineer for the rear wing on Bruin
Formula Racing, the goal was to find more downforce without paying for it in drag, and to place
the wing where it works in clean air.

## Approach

Five concepts were modeled in SolidWorks and run in STAR-CCM+:

- **Beam wings** — a lower element interacting with the diffuser flow
- **Endplate louvers** — relieving pressure at the tips to manage tip vortices
- **Endplate strakes** — conditioning flow along the endplate surfaces

Each concept was compared on lift and drag coefficients and on the flow structures behind it.

## Results

| Concept          | Feature studied                 | Outcome     |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Beam wing        | Lower-element interaction       | Placeholder |
| Endplate louvers | Tip vortex strength             | Placeholder |

The CFD showed that air disturbed by the driver's head was reaching the wing, so the mounting
height was raised by two inches in the current design.
